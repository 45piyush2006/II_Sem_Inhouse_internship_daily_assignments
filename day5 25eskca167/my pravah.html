❓ Frequently Asked Questions

When is PRAVAH 2026? ▼
PRAVAH 2026 is scheduled for July 10, 2026. Registration starts from today!
How do I register for events? ▼
You can register for events through our website registration form. Fill in your details, select your event, and submit. You'll receive confirmation on your email.
What are the entry fees? ▼
Entry fees vary by event. Gaming events range from ₹300-₹500, technical events are free or low-cost, and sports events are ₹500-₹600. Check individual event details for exact fees.
Can individuals participate in team events? ▼
Most team events require the specified number of members. However, we can help match you with other participants if you don't have a complete team. Contact us for more details.
What are the prize pools? ▼
Total prize pool exceeds ₹60,000! Top performers in each event will receive amazing prizes. Check the event details for specific prize information.
Is there a refund policy? ▼
Registrations are non-refundable. However, you can transfer your entry to another person if needed. Contact our support team for assistance.
